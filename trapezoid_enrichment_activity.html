Challenge 1: Supporting the School Stage

The front of a school stage has a trapezoid-shaped frame. Its two parallel bases measure 8 m and 16 m. A horizontal support beam connects the midpoints of the two legs.

Challenge 2: The Trapezoid Window

Challenge 3: Complete the Garden

A landscaped garden is shaped like a trapezoid. A pathway connecting the midpoints of its legs is 18 m long. One base measures 12 m, while the other base is unknown.

Hint: Use the trapezoid midsegment formula and solve for the unknown base.
